💡 What is TokenSlim?

TokenSlim is an AI prompt compressor that removes filler words, minifies instructions, and preserves important context. It works with Claude, GPT, Gemini and all coding agents (Continue.dev, OpenCode, Antigravity, VSCode).

💰 Every token saved = money saved. Long prompts waste 30-60% on filler. TokenSlim cuts the fat automatically.
